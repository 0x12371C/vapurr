Sponsored transactions

A relayer can pay your gas for you — you sign, it batches many people's signed requests into one on-chain call through VapurrForwarder, and pays the gas itself. Deployed and tested on RHC testnet. Not yet the default path for every payment in this build — KetPay still signs and pays directly today.

  • Repeat use is what makes this work. Your first sponsored transaction costs the relayer more than your wallet's later ones — a brand-new wallet's first use is never a real discount, at any batch size. After a handful of uses you're at the efficient, cheaper rate.
  • Sponsorship is a real cost, not a trick. Someone pays the gas either way — vapurr does, out of what this fee covers. The numbers above are the actual measured cost, not a marketing estimate.
  • On-chain, revocable authorization. Only a relayer address the contract owner has explicitly authorized can submit on your behalf — pulling that authorization takes one on-chain call, not a new build of this app.
