What is ZKKYC?

zer0ID uses zero-knowledge KYC: prove uniqueness and a few attributes without handing over a biometric database or raw photos. Durable state is nullifier-only — opaque in vapurrDB. Trust ladder L1–L4; browse-earn needs L4 ocular via open-iris. Builds on Worldcoin open-iris (MIT) and OpenAI technology where used — not official partnerships.

The zer0ID ladder (L1–L4)

Each rung proves one specific thing. Higher is not "more trusted" in general — it is a different claim. Order is /kyc/phone → /kyc/scan (open-iris).
