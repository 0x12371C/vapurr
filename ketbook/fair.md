# Fair

Same functions for every address. No whitelist. Virtual pool, 2% spread floor, 9% Lithe cap, and genesis amount are constants in the bytecode. Nobody retunes them after deploy.

The one unearned mint is genesis: the deploying device receives **1,000,000 $VAPURR**. After that, supply only moves when people mint or redeem $PUSD.

The remaining privilege is `feed`. The immutable owner posts a pending oracle. It cannot mint, cannot withdraw, cannot pause.

vapurrbid: first listing is 10 $PUSD. Taking #1 costs at least 5 more than the current top. Raises are the difference only. Same URL stays with the first bidder. Whole $PUSD, no refunds, bids do not expire.
