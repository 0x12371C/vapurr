# Lithe

The 9% on `$PUSD`. Not Earn (`vapurr://earn` is browse-to-earn).

`$PUSD` is shares times an index. Mint spread (≥ 2%) drips that index, capped at **9%**. Every holder is paid in place. No extra token. No claim tx.

Hold `$PUSD` because the browser spends it (KetPay, postage, vapurrbid). Lithe is why you do not have to flip it the same day.

`vapurr://lithe` opens the `$PUSD` desk.
