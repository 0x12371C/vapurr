# zzzmail

Mail in the app. Send to `@alice.hood` or an `0x`.

Postage is **0.25¢ $PUSD** (or `$VAPURR`). Gasless voucher. 0 ETH. The body is an IPFS CID, never on chain. If a relayer later posts a pointer, postage plus gas stays under a cent.

Letters live on this device until then. Send does not mean settled.
