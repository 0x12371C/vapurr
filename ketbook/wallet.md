# Wallet

Your money on this PC. Robinhood Chain.

**$PUSD** is the dollar you spend in the window. **$VAPURR** is what you burn to mint it. ETH is gas.

Open it from the rail or `vapurr://wallet`. Sign in once on this device. vapurr is not a bank.

Settings is the gear. Tap a token for its page. Receive is a QR. Send has Max, recents, and `name.hood`. Swap opens the quote chrome. This device signs. Network is testnet 46630 until you switch.

Live Trenches is a separate tab. It is not inside Wallet.
