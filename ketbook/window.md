# The window

Rail on the left. Tabs and the bar on top. The page is everything else.

Type a site, a `0x` address, a block, or `alice.hood`. `vapurr://` is a screen in the app.

Light mode is in settings. Builds sync through Settings → About (channel at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\vapurr\channel`). Apply stages `vapurr.next.exe` and relaunches. `pack.ps1` and `vapurr.exe --publish` stamp the channel.
